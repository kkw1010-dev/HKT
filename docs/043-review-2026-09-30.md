## Not changed

- **13, Surrender/Execute keys on switch-off:** by design. Prompt-only is its own panel switch per
  mod and switching it off gives the key back; an automatic restore on every module toggle would mean
  INI writes, a Valhalla reload and VM calls. Not a bug.
- **Surrender `baboController`:** the review is wrong; it is reset on every load where Acheron is
  present, and Acheron's presence cannot change within a session.
- **Needs/Deflate gamepad keys:** confirmed, but gamepad work is closed (the user, 2026-09-29).
- **Helmet `PutOn` base form:** confirmed as a code fact; which copy the engine equips is unverified
  and the fix (choosing the enchanted copy) is of low value. Left.
- **Squeeze gesture graph variables on load:** speculative; not changed.
